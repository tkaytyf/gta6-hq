Latest news

Nov 23, 2026

First patch, v01.002.002, is out

Rockstar's first post-launch update fixes crashes, weather that got stuck for long stretches, autosave failures, story phone calls that didn't trigger, a Wanted Level you couldn't shake, the gym deadlift bar, and partners not handing over bags for fences. Polygon reports roughly 604 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, rolling out in stages.

Sources: Polygon · Game Informer · GamesRadar+

Nov 23, 2026

Vintage Vice City Pack now sold separately for $5

The former pre-order bonus can now be bought on its own. It includes the '55 Vapid Stanier, a garage on Ocean Beach, plus outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia. Items unlock as the story progresses.

Source: GameSpot

Nov 23, 2026

GTA VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition)

Rockstar lists the Deluxe Edition of the soundtrack album on the official GTA VI page.

Source: Rockstar Games

Nov 20, 2026

$3.79 billion on day one

Take-Two says GTA VI made an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its first day, calling it a record for any entertainment launch, with 32.5 million units sold before release.

Sources: Take-Two · The Verge

Nov 19, 2026

GTA VI is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

The game is available now. US prices are $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate.

Sources: Rockstar Games · GTA 6 Everything (prices)

Status

PC version: not announced

Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date. Polygon has a report claiming a date in early 2027, but that is a report, not an announcement.

Sources: VGC · Polygon (report)

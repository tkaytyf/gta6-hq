The habits that save you money, progress and trophies early on, each with a source.

Before anything else

Set up good habits

Habit

Save by hand

There is no mission replay or chapter select. The game has 15 save slots, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. You can't save during a mission, in combat or in a chase.

Sources: Who Finished

Habit

Bank your cash

Dying or getting busted costs pocket cash, and shops only take up to $999 in cash. Before BuckMe unlocks, early mission pay lands in the mailbox outside Jason's house and you can stash cash under his floorboards.

Sources: CompletionMap · LootMapped

Habit

Carry a disguise

Police track your face, clothes, car, weapon and whether you travel as a couple. A cap and sunglasses, or a spare outfit in your trunk, clears those fast.

Sources: Polygon · All Things How

Chapter 1

What to do before Chapter 2

Money rule

Fill Family Savings

Starting Tourist Trap is the point of no return. Only Family Savings carries over, up to $5,000. Pocket cash, the floorboard stash, the house and your cars are lost.

Sources: GTA6.house · LootMapped

Weapons

Store your guns

Leave guns in the gun locker or your vehicle. You can buy them back cheaply at Gellhorn Outfitters in Chapter 2.

Sources: GTA6.house

Trophy

Do Lucia's balcony meditation

Positive Mental Attitude needs every meditation spot. The Chapter 1 spot at the house may be missable, so do it before you move on.

Sources: GTA 6 Encyclopedia · GTABase

Money

Sell to Donnie first

Donnie pays more than Brian for narcotics, and neither fence lasts the whole story. Donnie stops buying once Clear Skies starts.

Sources: HeistIntel · GTA6.house

Missions

Chapter 1-only optionals

Get Shirley's Rum, Ring the Bell! and Find the Lost Drugs (four packages worth about $900 each) are only offered in Chapter 1.

Sources: CutsceneWire · CompletionMap

Warning

Avoid the Irredeemable profile

GTABase warns that the Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent, so keep it in mind on a trophy run.

Sources: GTABase

Early cash

Easy early earners

Ch. 1

Gang hideouts

Raid Illegal Operations from Gone Fishing onward and fence what you take.

Sources: HeistIntel

Ch. 1

Kenton's treasure dives

Diving for treasure opens from Island Dreams.

Sources: HeistIntel

Any time

Cards & flamingos

Pawn shops buy collectible cards. Break Cocaine Flamingos and sell what's inside.

Sources: HeistIntel

Any time

Rob the robbers

Take loot off crews fleeing a crime: $1,000 to $15,000, best in Chapters 2 and 3.

Sources: PC Gamer

Cars

Scan before you steal

Hold up on the D-pad to open WAiNK. It shows a car's value, lock, alarm, tracker and registration cost.

Sources: Polygon · GameSpot

Wait

Don't rob banks yet

Bank cash comes as marked bills with dye packs. Wait for the jammer from Chapter 3's Credit Check.

Sources: Checkpoint Guides

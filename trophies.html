PS5 trophies

PS5 trophy
guide

55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver and 46 bronze. All single-player, about 200 hours to platinum.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Roadmap

Road to platinum

Step

1 · Story first

Story trophies pop as you play. Take optional missions as they appear, scan cars and study animals along the way.

Sources: GTABase

Step

2 · Don't miss this

Do Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude.

Sources: GTABase · GTA 6 Encyclopedia

Step

3 · 100% checklist

After the credits, work through Progress › Stats › 100% Completion. Cheats turn off trophies and saving.

Sources: GTABase · Who Finished

    The rules

    Money rules

    🔒 Chapter 1 → 2: what you keep minor spoiler

    Once you start the mission Tourist Trap, you can't turn back. When Chapter 1 ends you lose your pocket cash, your BuckMe checking balance, the cash under Jason's floorboards, the Key Lento house and every personal vehicle. GTA6.house CompletionMap

    1. Only Family Savings carries over, up to $5,000. Before Tourist Trap, deposit at an ATM, then in the BuckMe app use Buck Yourself to move money into Family Savings. You can never withdraw it. GTA6.house
    2. Store your guns in the gun locker or your own vehicle. You can get them back cheaply at Gellhorn Outfitters in Chapter 2. CompletionMap, citing IGN
    3. Sell stolen goods first. Donnie only buys until Clear Skies starts, and Brian is at his Key Lento lockup. CompletionMap, citing IGN
    4. Expect to start Chapter 2 with just a few hundred dollars. CompletionMap, citing IGN

    Sources disagree: GTA6.house says unique weapons you've collected carry over, and player reports on Reddit say weapon purchases and upgrades do too. IGN's advice is to store guns in the locker or your vehicle. Doing that is the safe choice either way.

    Bank everything

    If you're Wasted or Busted, you can lose the cash you're carrying. IGN and Push Square say you lose all of it, while PC Gamer says a few hundred dollars. You can carry up to $20,000. Shops take pocket cash only for buys up to $999, and BuckMe pays for anything bigger.

    CompletionMap · GTA6.house

    Rob other criminals

    When you see a crew fleeing a robbery, take them down and grab their bags. PC Gamer reports $1,000 to $15,000, and it works especially well in Chapters 2 and 3.

    PC Gamer

    Steal cars for Moses & Randy

    From Chapter 2, Moses buys older cars without trackers. VGC reports an average of $1,000 to $5,000, and there's a cooldown between sales. From Chapter 3, Randy pays more for flashy cars. PC Gamer reports $1,000 to $20,000 overall.

    PC Gamer · CompletionMap

    Demolition Derby

    Ambrosia Raceway pays over $3,000 for a win on the oval and about double on the figure-eight, and you can repeat it. PC Gamer reports $3,800 to $6,300.

    PC Gamer

    Back-door robberies

    The snap gun you get in Chapter 3 picks locks. Go in the back, kill the alarm and cameras, then clear the safe. PC Gamer reports $6,000 to $15,000.

    PC Gamer

    Pilot Pete's drops

    Timed air-drop jobs that open up in Chapter 4. PC Gamer reports $10,000 to $20,000 per run.

    PC Gamer

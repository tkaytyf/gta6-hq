Time & weather

Weather & night secrets

Weather

Hurricanes

Locked behind Freddie's secret mission at a prepper bunker in North Leonard County. Break in once, wait for his text, then beat his upgraded defenses. After that, hurricanes can hit at any time.

Sources: GameSpot

See on map

Night

After 2 AM: the UFO

A random night event near the Starlet Motel in Kelly County. Go alone and keep trying if nothing shows.

Sources: Leonipedia · OpenLeonida

Night

Midnight to 4 AM: Circles of Hell

Two metalheads in a northern Port Gellhorn garage take you on a trip that ends with the Hellspawn Comshot 12 and a max-health boost.

Sources: OpenLeonida

Night

Night only: Kirk's quests

The Skunk Ape quest line only runs at night.

Sources: Leonipedia

Night

Night: meteorites glow

Meteorite chunks glow in the dark, so hunt them at night.

Sources: The Big Lead

Patch

Weather bug fixed

Patch 01.002.002 fixed weather that stopped changing for long periods.

Sources: IGN

    Money rule

    Chapter 1 → 2:
    what you keep

    Starting the mission Tourist Trap is the point of no return. When Chapter 1 ends you lose your pocket cash, your BuckMe checking balance, the floorboard stash, the Key Lento house and your cars.

    • Only Family Savings carries over, up to $5,000. Deposit at an ATM, then use Buck Yourself in BuckMe before Tourist Trap. You can't withdraw it.
    • Store guns in the gun locker or your vehicle. Get them back cheaply at Gellhorn Outfitters in Chapter 2.
    • Sell stolen goods first. Donnie only buys until Clear Skies starts.

    Sources: GTA6.house · CompletionMap

    Tip line · Call 02Verified

    Money tip

    Bank it,
    don't carry it

    You can carry up to $20,000, and getting Wasted or Busted costs you cash. IGN and Push Square say you lose all of it, while PC Gamer says a few hundred dollars. Shops take pocket cash only up to $999, and BuckMe pays for anything bigger. Deposit after every job.

    Outlets disagree on how much cash you lose when you die.

    Sources: CompletionMap · GTA6.house

    Tip line · Call 03Verified

    Money tip

    Rob the
    robbers

    When you hear alarms or see a crew fleeing with duffel bags, take them down and grab the loot. PC Gamer reports $1,000 to $15,000, and it pays best in Chapters 2 and 3. Play as both Jason and Lucia so you can carry every bag to a fence.

    Other earners PC Gamer reports: the Ambrosia Raceway Demolition Derby ($3,800 to $6,300 per win), back-door store robberies with the Chapter 3 snap gun ($6,000 to $15,000) and Pilot Pete's drop runs in Chapter 4 ($10,000 to $20,000).

    Sources: PC Gamer

    Best cars

    Cars the
    fences want

    From Chapter 2, Moses buys older, low-profile cars. From Chapter 3, Randy pays more for flashy, high-end ones. GTA 6 Map pins these cars for Randy:

    • Pegassi '96 Infernus · Little Cuba
    • Bravado Banshee · Peacock Bay
    • Piccia Furia · Peacock Bay
    • Granforma Zorrusso · Ocean Beach
    • Invetero Coquette D10 · Watson Island
    • Pegassi Vitello · North Beaches
    • Ascot Paragon · Belville
    • Pegassi Tempesta · Fairyland Forest
    • Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire · Hamlet

    Each pin is an area to search, not a guaranteed spawn. Open all 101 cars →

    Sources: GTA 6 Map · PC Gamer

    Weapons

    Free guns
    near the Keys

    • Couch Potato Mustang .357: trailer home off Tanglewood Ln, Port Gellhorn. Shoot down the sofa hanging in a tree, then sit on it.
    • Unpacked Comshot 12: north-west coast house in Key Lento, behind the upstairs en-suite door. A note in the kitchen hints at it.
    • Assassin Nipper .38: floor hatch on a shipwreck at Permit Keys' south-east tip. It comes suppressed.
    • Smuggler Gorse 425: on a beached smuggler boat east of the air station.

    GTA Town says 6 free weapons only appear during missions and can be missed, because there's no mission replay. Open all 73 weapons →

    Sources: GTA Town · Fextralife

    Tip line · Call 06Verified

    Hidden secrets

    Puzzles that
    hide loot

    • Big Pine bunker: the Cold War 556 is tied to a hidden-hatch puzzle.
    • Pelican Valley research outpost: solve the puzzle for the door code to reach the Crabshack VF5.
    • Black Eagle warehouse, Balsero: read the office safe code off the PC. A gun case nearby sits behind a gap only Lucia fits through.
    • Hellspawn Comshot 12: GTA Town lists it as a challenge reward at a carved wooden throne on a hilltop campsite in North Kelly County. GTAVIMaps says it comes from a trip to Hell and a boss fight at the Circles of Hell House in northern Port Gellhorn after midnight. The guides disagree on where it starts.

    Sources: GTA Town · GTAVIMaps

    Tip line · Call 07Verified

    Easter eggs

    Leonida's
    easter eggs

    • Radio tower egg: climb the tallest mast in Vice-Dale County to a giant nest of painted eggs. Throw one off for a short cutscene.
    • UFO: after midnight east of Port Gellhorn, follow the blue glow and you'll be beamed up, unlocking the trophy The Truth Is Out There.
    • Bigfoot: a growl, an effigy and a blackout in the swamps. One guide places it south of Cottonmouth Lake, another around Watson Bay and Shell Bayou.
    • Cool Dog: get stranded at sea and a dog on a jet ski rescues you. GTA Vice Guides says a dog later digs up a wallet with $1,218.
    • Dexter's apartment: a building in Vice City modeled on the TV show's.

    An alien bunker with keypad code 2314 is reported by one guide only, so it's unverified.

    Sources: GTAVIMaps · GTA Vice Guides · Polygon

    Tip line · Call 08Verified

    Mission tips

    Don't lose
    progress

    • There's no mission replay. Make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.
    • Four optional missions can be missed for good, though none is needed for a trophy.
    • Chapter 1 only: Get Shirley's Rum, Ring the Bell! and Find the Lost Drugs. That's four packages of about $900 of cocaine each.
    • If a story call won't come, pass time with side activities and install patch 01.002.002, which fixes calls that didn't trigger.

    Open all 91 missions (spoiler-gated) →

    Sources: CutsceneWire · CompletionMap · GamesRadar+

    Tip line · Call 09Verified

    Collectible clues

    Clues worth
    following

    • Talk to the parrot: at the Exotic Animals Front behind a liquor store, deal with the guards and the parrot tells you where the cash stash is hidden.
    • Pawn shops buy collectibles: collectible cards, meteorites and doubloons. The closest early shop is in Hamlet.
    • Check rooftops and the coast: rooftop lockboxes hold small amounts of cash, and washed-up boats sometimes carry cocaine bricks.
    • Meteorite crater: a campsite on Mount Kalaga's east side has one to pick up. What it's used for is still unconfirmed.

    Full collectible maps are coming soon.

    Sources: CompletionMap · PC Gamer · GTA Vice Guides

    Bonus content

    Vercetti nods
    & bonuses

The Vintage Vice City Pack, now $5 on its own, adds a palm-print pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's shirt. Ultimate Edition activities include Wyman's Classic Car Collection, which opens in Chapter 2.

    Cops & wanted level

    Cops &
    wanted level

    Stars show how hard they're looking. Heat icons show what they know.

    Last updated November 24, 20261 min read

    On this page
    What the stars meanClear what they knowGetting away

    Stars

    What the stars mean

    StarsWhat it means
    TransparentA crime was reported, but the suspect is unknown.
    WhitePolice see you and know who you are.
    FlashingYou're losing them.
    GrayThey lost sight of you but are still searching. Change clothes, car or look now.
    RedYou're wanted inside the red area on the map.

    One star sends one car, two stars several, and three brings an armed helicopter. Six is the maximum.

    Sources: Polygon

    Heat icons

    Clear what they know

    IconPolice haveHow to clear it
    HeadYour appearanceCap and sunglasses, a haircut, or a mask put on before the crime
    HangerYour outfitChange your whole outfit, including a spare from your trunk
    CameraCCTV footageDestroy the footage inside the building
    CarYour vehicleDitch it, or respray at Pay 'n' Spray
    CoupleTwo suspects togetherSplit Jason and Lucia up
    GunYour weaponHide or drop it

    Sources: Polygon · All Things How

    Escape

    Getting away

    Step 1

    Leave the zone

    Break line of sight and get out of the red search area. Forests, bridges and buildings help.

    Sources: Polygon

    Step 2

    Then clear icons

    Clearing one icon doesn't clear the others. Work through each.

    Sources: All Things How

    Patch

    Bug fixed

    Patch 01.002.002 fixed a bug where you couldn't lose your wanted level.

    Sources: IGN

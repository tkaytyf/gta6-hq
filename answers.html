    Common questions

    What carries over to Chapter 2 in GTA 6?

    AnswerVerified

    Only Family Savings carries past Tourist Trap, up to $5,000. Bank it before you start that mission.

    Sources: GTA6.house

    Link to this answer

    Do you lose your guns after Chapter 1?

    AnswerVerified

    No. Stored guns aren't lost. You can buy them back cheaply at Gellhorn Outfitters in Chapter 2.

    Sources: GTA6.house

    Link to this answer

    Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

    AnswerVerified

    No. There's no mission replay, so make a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.

    Sources: Who Finished

    Link to this answer

    How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

    AnswerVerified

    55, and none of them need online play.

    Sources: GTABase

    Link to this answer

    Do properties make money in GTA 6?

    AnswerVerified

    No. No property pays an income, and garages are just parking spaces.

    Sources: LootMapped

    Link to this answer

    Why can't I sell bank cash to a fence?

    AnswerVerified

    Banks pay in marked bills with dye packs. You need the jammer from Credit Check before a fence will buy them.

    Sources: Checkpoint Guides

    Link to this answer

    How do you unlock hurricanes in GTA 6?

    AnswerVerified

    Hurricanes stay locked until you finish Freddie's secret bunker mission.

    Sources: GameSpot

    Link to this answer

    When does the UFO appear in GTA 6?

    AnswerVerified

    Late at night, after 2 AM, near the Starlet Motel in Kelly County.

    Sources: Leonipedia

    Link to this answer

    How do you lose the police description fast?

    AnswerVerified

    A cap and sunglasses can clear the police's description of you, so keep a set handy.

    Sources: Polygon

    Link to this answer

    Where is the hidden Vault Radio station?

    AnswerVerified

    Scan the pink gas-mask poster in The Vault nightclub to unlock it.

    Sources: GameSpot

    Link to this answer

    When should you look for meteorite chunks?

    AnswerVerified

    At night. Meteorite chunks glow in the dark.

    Sources: The Big Lead

    Link to this answer

    Is GTA 6 Online out?

    AnswerVerified

    Rockstar hasn't announced a release date yet, so it's TBA.

    Sources: Rockstar

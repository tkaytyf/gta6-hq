    Add-ons

    DLC & bonus content

    Available

    Vintage Vice City Pack

    A '55 Vapid Stanier with a garage on Ocean Beach, plus '80s outfits, hairstyles and a palm-print pattern that nods to Tommy Vercetti's shirt. It came free with pre-orders and now costs $5 on its own.

    GameSpot · Rockstar

    Available

    Ultimate Edition extras

    Outfits, vehicles and weapons spread across the story, plus two extra activities: the PTT Youngin$ compound raid and Wyman's Classic Car Collection. Sources mention the '95 Grotti Cheetah unlocking in Chapter 5 and Wyman's collection opening in Chapter 2.

    GameSpot · GamesRadar+ · GTA 6 Everything

    Coming soon

    Story DLC / expansions

    Nothing announced as of Nov 24, 2026. Rockstar's Stephen Donkin told Kotaku a few map areas leave "options in the future". That's a comment on design, not an announcement. We'll add expansions here once Rockstar confirms any.

    GTA 6 Everything · Leonipedia

    Coming soon

    GTA 6 Online

    Not announced. Take-Two calls GTA VI a single-player game and says Rockstar will keep supporting the existing GTA Online. Talk of a 2027 date comes from reports, not from Rockstar.

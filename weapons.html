All 73 weapons

All 73 free named weapons, summarized from GTA Town (checked Nov 24) and cross-checked against Fextralife. "Gang op loot" means it's in the stash of a gang's illegal operation.

Counts differ: GTA Town lists 73 free named weapons, while gta6map.io tracks 102 unique-weapon locations. Story-mission weapons are hidden behind a spoiler toggle, and GTA Town says they can be missed because there's no mission replay. Per-weapon stats are coming soon.

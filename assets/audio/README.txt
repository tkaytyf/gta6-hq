Gatorline swamp sounds
======================
swamp.ogg / swamp.m4a: a 23-second seamless loop of swamp ambience (water bed, crickets, frogs).
Source: self-generated by synthesis with make_swamp.py (numpy, no recorded samples, no third-party audio).
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication) by tkay / Gatorline.
Off by default; plays only after the visitor turns on the "Swamp sounds" toggle. Choice is remembered in localStorage.

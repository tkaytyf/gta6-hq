Characters

Rockstar's eight official profiles, summarized in our own words, with the cast from the game's credits.

Last updated November 24, 2026

Jump toMain charactersKey side characters

Official profiles

Main characters

Played by Dylan Rourke

Jason Duval

Raised around grifters, he joined the Army to escape a rough youth and ended up running jobs for drug smugglers in the Keys. He wants an easy life; meeting Lucia could change everything.

Played by Manni L. Perez

Lucia Caminos

Taught to fight by her father, she went to Leonida Penitentiary for protecting her family. Out now, she wants the good life her mother dreamed of since their Liberty City days, and has a plan.

Played by Bobby Moynihan

Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and a fellow associate of Brian's. Happiest at home with a few beers, listening in on Coast Guard radio.

Played by Mike Forbs

Boobie Ike

A Vice City legend who turned street money into real estate, a strip club and a studio. His big bet is Only Raw Records with Dre'Quan.

Played by Naiqui Macabroad

Dre'Quan Priest

More hustler than gangster, he always wanted to break into music. He has signed Real Dimez and is aiming at the Vice City scene.

Played by Hood Brat (Bae-Luxe), Krissy Celess (Roxy)

Real Dimez

A rap duo and friends since high school. An early hit with DWNPLY made them, and now they're signed to Only Raw Records.

Played by Manny Perez

Raul Bautista

A confident, charming veteran bank robber who recruits people willing to take big risks. His recklessness raises the stakes.

Played by Stephen Root

Brian Heder

An old-school Keys drug runner who still moves product through his boat yard with his wife Lori. He lets Jason live rent-free in exchange for help.

Sources: Polygon · VGC

Hunting & fishing

How to hunt and fish

Hunting

Meet Bret

Hunting jobs come from Bret Vogel near Mount Kalaga. He texts Jason after Coffee Break in Chapter 2. His first job teaches hunting.

Sources: GTA Playbook

Hunting

One clean shot

A single shot to the head or heart keeps the carcass pristine. For a gator or bear, send Bret a photo and location and he collects it.

Sources: GTA Playbook

Fishing

Fishing basics

Cast, reel slowly to tempt a bite, then tire the fish by pulling against it. Each catch goes in your log.

Sources: GameSpot

Trophy

Beat Cal

Cal's fishing challenge unlocks Baited My Tackle.

Sources: GTABase

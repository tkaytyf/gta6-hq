Interactive map

    Schematic, not to scale. Regions sit on the grid squares of Rockstar's official Leonida map as read by MyGTA. Pins mark the region to search, not an exact spot: open a pin for the directions. Exact-coordinate pins are coming soon. Map library: Leaflet (BSD-2).

    Sources: MyGTA · OpenLeonida · GTA VI Companion

    Regions

    The six regions

    G7

    Vice City

    The big city: Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market and the VC Port. The story moves here in Chapter 3.

    Sources: GTA VI Companion · OpenLeonida

    K6–L3

    Leonida Keys

    The island chain where the game begins, from Key Lento to Mile Zero Air Station.

    Sources: MyGTA · OpenLeonida

    I4–I5

    Grassrivers

    Wetlands of gators, mangroves and airboats. Watson Bay is the main town; Pelican Valley is Skunk Ape country.

    Sources: MyGTA · OpenLeonida

    E3

    Port Gellhorn

    "Leonida's forgotten coast": cheap motels and closed attractions. The Chapter 2 base.

    Sources: GTA VI Companion · OpenLeonida

    E5–E6

    Ambrosia

    The industrial heart: the Allied Crystal sugar refinery, a biker gang and Ambrosia Raceway Park.

    Sources: GTA VI Companion · OpenLeonida

    C5

    Mount Kalaga

    A national park on the northern border for hunting, fishing and off-road trails.

    Sources: GTA VI Companion · OpenLeonida
